AI4Mars · Curiosity NavCam

Mars terrain segmentation

A U-Net with a ResNet34 encoder labels every pixel of a rover image as soil, bedrock, sand or big rock. The model runs in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web, so your images are never uploaded.
